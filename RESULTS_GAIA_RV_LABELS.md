# Gaia DR3 RV-amplitude labels: result (8 Nov 2026, run once per `GAIA_RV_LABEL_PLAN.md`)

- **Sample:** 487 candidates have ≥ 6 RVS transits and a published `rv_amplitude_robust`.
- **Positives:** only **2** exceed the pre-specified substellar limit (2·K80 + 5σ + 1 km/s; median K80 = 2.4 km/s).
- **Status:** NOT_CONDUCTED:BELOW_MIN_N (the plan needs ≥ 5). No AUC was computed.
- **Reading:**
  - Gaia's epoch RV noise for these stars is comparable to the substellar signal, so the conservative noise margin leaves almost no positives.
  - Unresolved near-twins also move the RV centroid little (label blindness).
  - A larger independent RV label set needs multi-epoch survey RVs: LAMOST-MRS time-domain, APOGEE visits (already used), or follow-up.
