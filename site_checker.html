Is this Gaia “planet” really a binary star?

Gaia’s astrometric orbits list thousands of stars that seem to wobble because of a planet or brown dwarf. Many are impostors: two stars whose combined light hides the second one. GAIA-FP scores every candidate with Gaia data alone. The scores were frozen before Gaia DR4 (2 December 2026) and will be tested blind against it.
