## Remaining blockers — not silently patched

1. The full `dr3_astrospectro_all.csv` is absent. The schema suite now tests the available joined 95-row subset, but this does not restore the missing full population or validate population-level completeness.
2. `src/dr4_fetch.py` has **not** been exercised against the live Gaia Archive in this environment. Its actual TAP_SCHEMA query, release-specific columns, authentication and DataLink retrieval remain unverified. A local test cannot substitute for a live archive fetch.
3. DR4's final release schema and DataLink products must be checked against the actual release on 2 December. Pre-release column assumptions are not proof of release compatibility.
4. H6 runner and H4 DR4 epoch-astrometry adapter remain open items in `DR4_READINESS.md`; they must either be implemented and tested before unblinding or explicitly marked NOT CONDUCTED under the registered rules.
5. The mass–luminosity relation/isochrone assumptions, mock-catalogue realism, correlated Thiele–Innes errors, and bootstrap p-value calibration remain outside this completed test suite, as stated in `AUDIT_REPORT.md`.

## Interpretation

This revision closes the four environment-dependent schema test failures by making the fixture honest and reproducible. It does **not** establish that the live DR4 fetch works, that all registered hypotheses are executable, or that GAIA-FP will be supported by DR4. Do not describe the project as flawless or fully DR4-ready on the basis of these tests.
