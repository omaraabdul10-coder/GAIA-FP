## 1. To a Gaia binary / impostor expert (e.g. the Mazeh–Shahaf group, or the authors of arXiv:2305.08623)

**Subject:** High-school pre-registered blind test of Gaia DR4 substellar candidates — a short question

Dear Dr. [Name],

We are two high-school students from Ganja, Azerbaijan, working on a project for the Regeneron ISEF.

Your paper on [their paper] motivated our question: can Gaia's own data separate binary impostors from genuine substellar companions? We want to answer it with a test whose rules are fixed **before** Gaia DR4 exists.

What we have done:
- We froze our method and hypotheses on OSF ([10.17605/OSF.IO/TBJGY](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/TBJGY); a further amendment is planned for 19 Nov).
- We will run it once on DR4.

On DR3 (development data only) we found:
- (i) RVS line broadening adds information beyond the CMD (ΔAUC = +0.044 [+0.033, +0.055], n = 1927, labels from a0 vs a1 sin i).
- (ii) The excess follows a per-system blend term, which needs both light ratio and velocity separation.
- (iii) When the lines are unresolved, the RV follows the light centroid, so ρ → 1 and luminous blends look dark. We are calling this "label blindness".

Our question: does (iii) agree with your experience, or is there a known treatment we should cite?

Thank you for any comment, however brief.
